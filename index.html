Voxelia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Voxelia mobile game ("the game", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Voxelia is developed by Wasilisk.

In short: the game itself has no accounts, does not ask for your name, email or location, and does not run its own servers. Your puzzle progress is stored on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve those ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The game keeps a save file on your device with your puzzle progress (solved levels, stars, unfinished grids, unlocked packs, the hints you have left, which tips you have seen, the day you first opened the Daily Hall) and your settings (music, sound, haptics, language, graphics and similar options). This file stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example to get extra hints. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's Advertising ID (on Android, the Google advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location it implies;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped or finished them;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance information from the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and improve ads, to prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to show personalised ads. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can choose whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. If you decline, ads may still be shown, but they are not personalised. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's consent SDK. To change it, you can clear the game's data or reinstall the game, and the form will be shown again.

4. Google Play Games (when available)

Some versions of the game may let you sign in to Google Play Games to back up your progress to the cloud and keep it in sync across devices. If you use this feature, the game reads your Play Games player profile to sign you in and stores a copy of your save file in your Google Play Games Saved Games, under your Google account. This data is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your email address. You can delete the saved game data from your Google account at any time through the Google Play Games settings.

5. Google Play

When you install the game from Google Play, Google may share anonymous crash reports and app performance data (Android vitals) with us through the Google Play Console. This data does not identify you.

6. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information, and we do not sell personal information. The game has no in-app purchases at this time and no chat or social features.

8. Children

Voxelia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data security and retention

Data sent to Google is transmitted over encrypted connections and kept according to Google's own retention policies. Data stored on your device remains until you delete the game or its data.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data that identifies you, most requests are best directed to Google through your Google Account. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example when a new feature or service is added. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
